About Mango

A studio built around real collaboration

We started Mango because too many good ideas get lost in translation between strategy, design and code. So we kept the team small, senior, and all under one roof.

Our story

From a two-person side project to a studio clients keep coming back to.

Mango began in a small apartment with one laptop and a shared belief: good design should feel inevitable, not decorated. What started as freelance work for local shops grew, project by project, into a full studio.

Today we work with founders, growing brands and the occasional big-name client who wants something that doesn't look like it came from a template. Every project still gets the same close attention the first one did.
