Seventeen years of building curious minds

Abdullah Academy started with two classrooms and a simple idea: children learn best when they're trusted to explore. That idea still runs the school today.

Our Story

From one small campus to a community of 1,200 learners

Founded in 2009 in Gulberg, Lahore, Abdullah Academy began as a single-block school for 60 students. Today we run three campuses across the city, but the founding promise hasn't changed — every child is known by name, and every teacher is chosen for how much they care, not just what they know.

We believe a great school day mixes rigorous academics with real curiosity: a science experiment that gets messy, a debate that runs long, a painting that doesn't turn out how you planned. That's where real learning happens.
