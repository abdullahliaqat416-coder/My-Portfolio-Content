Common Questions

Frequently asked by parents

What is the admissions process?+

Submit the enquiry form, attend a placement assessment, then a short interview with the family. Most decisions are shared within a week.

Do you offer transport and lunch?+

Yes — door-to-door van routes cover most of Lahore, and a supervised cafeteria serves fresh lunch daily.

Are scholarships available?+

Merit and need-based scholarships are offered each year. Ask our admissions counselor for eligibility details.

Can I schedule a campus tour before applying?+

Absolutely. Use the "Book a Tour" button anywhere on the site, or call our front desk directly.
