Marketplace Philosophy

Built to earn their place.

OBJECT MARKET is an independent marketplace curating functional objects, everyday tools, audio instruments, and architectural lighting from world-class design studios.

Rigorous Curation

We test every object for mechanical durability, tactile honesty, and repairability. We believe that tools on your desk and objects in your home should outlast consumer electronics cycles.

Studio Direct Partnerships

By partnering directly with small-batch manufacturers in Tokyo, Berlin, San Francisco and Stockholm, we guarantee authentic provenance, fair maker compensation, and verified warranty coverage.
