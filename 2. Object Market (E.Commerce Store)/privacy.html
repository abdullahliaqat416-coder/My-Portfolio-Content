Privacy Policy

Data Integrity

OBJECT MARKET complies with GDPR and CCPA standards. We only process information required to fulfill orders and provide technical support.

Encrypted Transactions

All payments are handled via 256-bit TLS encrypted channels. Payment card data is processed directly by PCI-compliant merchant processors and is never retained on our systems.

Cookies & Telemetry

Cookies are used solely for session authentication, shopping cart persistence, and essential functional preferences.
