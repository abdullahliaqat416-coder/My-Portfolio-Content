Frequently Asked Questions

Are all products covered by manufacturer warranties?

Yes. As an authorized distributor, all products sold through OBJECT MARKET include full original studio and manufacturer warranties (typically 2 to 5 years depending on the brand).

How fast do orders ship?

Orders placed before 13:00 PST ship the same business day from our central distribution hub in California. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

Can I return an item?

We offer a 30-day return window for items in new, original condition with all packaging, manuals, and accessories included.
