The Biella Wool Tradition: Inside the Italian Mill

High in the foothills of the Italian Alps, alpine glacier runoff and generational mastery create the world's most supple double-faced boiled wool.

Biella has been the beating heart of European luxury textiles since the early thirteenth century. What distinguishes this valley from any other wool-producing region is its extraordinarily soft water, naturally filtered through granite rocks.

The Geometry of Double-Faced Weaving

Our Form Wool Overshirt utilizes a complex double-cloth construction. Two separate layers of superfine virgin wool yarn are woven simultaneously on double-rapier looms, interlocked by invisible binder threads. The result is structural heft without stiff rigidity.

Designed to Endure

Boiled wool retains the natural lanolin oils of the fleece, giving it gentle weather resistance and temperature regulation. It is a material that resists wrinkling and gains an ever-softer hand as the years pass.
