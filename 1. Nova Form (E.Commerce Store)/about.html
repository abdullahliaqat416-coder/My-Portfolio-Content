Studio Origin

Objects for the way you live.

Founded on the belief that everyday wardrobe essentials should possess quiet architectural integrity, tactile honesty, and lifelong longevity.

The Philosophy of Reduction

At NOVA FORM, our design process starts with stripping away superfluous adornment. We focus entirely on silhouette, the drape of virgin wool, the patina of vegetable-tanned saddle leather, and the tactile balance of clean seams.

Honest Materials

Every textile is traced to ethical mills in Italy, Portugal and Scotland. We work with double-faced virgin wools, raw Belgian linens, and certified organic cottons that soften and gain character through years of authentic wear.

Our Studios

Our creative studio operates between Copenhagen and Milan. We produce in limited batch editions to prevent overproduction and preserve artisanal intimacy.
