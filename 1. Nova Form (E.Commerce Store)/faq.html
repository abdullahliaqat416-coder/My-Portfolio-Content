Frequently Asked Questions

Guidance regarding ordering, our materials, care, and worldwide shipping.

Where are NOVA FORM pieces manufactured?+

All garments and leather objects are manufactured in specialized heritage ateliers across Northern Italy and Portugal, working exclusively with natural, traceable raw fibers.

How do I determine my size?+

Our garments follow contemporary European sizing. Pieces designed for relaxed layering (such as the Form Wool Overshirt) incorporate 3–4cm of ease. If you prefer a tailored fit, choose your standard size; if you prefer an oversized aesthetic, size up.

What are your shipping rates and times?+

We offer complimentary carbon-neutral express delivery on all orders over $200. Orders ship within 1–2 business days via DHL Express.

What is your return policy?+

Items in unworn condition with original tags and packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.
