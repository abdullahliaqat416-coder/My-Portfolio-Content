Studio Returns & Exchanges

We stand behind the craftsmanship of every piece with a thoughtful 30-day return policy.

Initiating a Return

To request a return or exchange, simply email concierge@novaform.com with your order number. A prepaid return courier shipping label will be generated and sent directly to your email.

Condition Requirements

Garments must be returned unwashed, unworn, and with all original studio tags intact. Footwear must be tried on carpeted surfaces only.

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 3–5 business days following physical inspection at our Copenhagen studio.
