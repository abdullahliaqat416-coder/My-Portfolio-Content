Shipping & Delivery

All pieces are inspected, hand-packed in archival unbleached paper, and dispatched directly from our Paris atelier or Kyoto studio.

Orders within France and the European Union are dispatched via DHL Express or Colissimo. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days. Express next-day courier service is available at checkout for orders placed before 13:00 CET.

Complimentary carbon-neutral shipping applies automatically to all orders exceeding $300.

We deliver worldwide via DHL Express International (3–5 business days to North America, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East). All shipments are fully insured against loss or transit damage.

Taxes and duties: For the United States, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Japan, duties and import taxes are calculated and collected at checkout (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid), ensuring no unexpected fees upon arrival.

Ceramic and blown glass works are cradled in custom molded biodegradable wool cushions and double-walled timber-reinforced boxes. Each package is individually numbered and signed by our shipping master.

As soon as your parcel is collected by the courier, you will receive a tracking link via email with live GPS milestones. For special delivery requirements or white-glove courier arrangements, please contact concierge@atelierlumen.com.
