A Note on Wear

Waxed canvas will crease, mark and darken at the corners with genuine use — this is the material doing exactly what it is meant to do. Leather is vegetable-tanned and will deepen in colour over a year of travel. We offer a re-waxing service for canvas pieces; write to us through Contact.
