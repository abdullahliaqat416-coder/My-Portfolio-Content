Client Care

Returns & Exchanges

We want every piece you welcome into your life to be cherished. If an item does not suit your space or silhouette, we provide a seamless 30-day return experience.

Full-price garments, leather goods, and homewares in unused, pristine condition with original tags and archival packaging may be returned within 30 days of receipt for a full refund or exchange.

Numbered limited edition sculptures, made-to-order furniture, and personalized monograms are crafted specifically for the collector and are considered final sale unless an error occurred in production.

1. Contact our client concierge at returns@atelierlumen.com with your order reference number.

2. We will issue a pre-paid DHL return label and arrange a scheduled courier collection from your residence or studio.

3. Once received and inspected at our Paris studio, refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 3 business days.
