Our Beginning

Founded on a Simple Complaint

Atelier Lumen began in 2016 in a single room above a tannery in Paris, out of frustration with how quickly beautiful things fell apart. Our founder, Amélie Rousseau, had spent a decade in luxury textile design before deciding to make fewer things, made properly, instead.

What started as a six-piece linen capsule sold from a shared studio table has grown, slowly and by design, into a small house working across wear, objects, travel and home — always in the same unhurried way.

Our Practice

Two Ateliers, One Standard

We now work from two small ateliers — one in Paris for textiles and leather, one in Kyoto for ceramics and glass — connected by the same design language and the same refusal to rush a finish.

Fewer than forty pieces leave either workshop in a typical week. We consider this a feature, not a limitation.
