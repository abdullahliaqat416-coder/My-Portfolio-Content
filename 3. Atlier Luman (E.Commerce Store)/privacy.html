Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

We treat your personal information with the same discretion, restraint, and care that define our craft.

1. Information We Collect

When you acquire a piece from Atelier Lumen or join our correspondence, we collect only the necessary details required to fulfill your order and communicate with you: your name, shipping address, billing address, email, and telephone number. We do not store payment card numbers on our servers.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your details are used strictly for order fulfillment, dispatch updates, and studio correspondence. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party marketers under any circumstances.

3. Studio Correspondence

Our newsletters are infrequent and strictly focused on archival releases, workshop exhibitions, and new edition announcements. You may unsubscribe at any time with a single click in any email footer.

4. Your Rights

Under GDPR and international privacy legislation, you have the right to request access to your data, demand correction, or request complete deletion of your records. Please contact privacy@atelierlumen.com to make an inquiry.
