Fifteen years of flour, fire and family

What started in one rented kitchen is now a bakery three generations of Lahoris have grown up on.

Our beginning

A bakery built before sunrise

Abdullah Bakery opened its doors in 2011 in a single rented kitchen in Liberty Market. Every loaf was mixed by hand, proofed overnight, and delivered by bicycle before the city woke up.

Word spread fast — not because of marketing, but because the bread tasted like someone actually cared. Fifteen years on, that's still the only recipe we follow.
