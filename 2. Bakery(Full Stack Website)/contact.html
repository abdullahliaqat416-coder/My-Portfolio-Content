Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?+

At least 48 hours for standard designs, and 5 days for elaborate multi-tier cakes.

Do you deliver across Lahore?+

Yes — most orders are delivered within an hour of confirmation, citywide.

How do baking classes work?+

Small weekend groups of 6–8 people, all ingredients and equipment included, hosted at our Liberty Market kitchen.

Can you cater for allergies or dietary needs?+

We offer eggless and reduced-sugar options — just mention it in your order message.
